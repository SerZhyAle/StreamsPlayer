Window help

Help

Every window with help has a ? button near its top-right corner and answers F1. These pages are what they open, in your language, and they work with no internet.

How to ask

Press F1 in any window, or click the ? button. The page of that window opens in your web browser; the application stays open and nothing in it changes.

In Settings the page opens at the section you are looking at. With a control focused that has its own entry, F1 opens that entry.
