# Six, not seven, and a deliberate script mix. Indian Windows users type Latin script or plain English
# into the Store constantly even with a Devanagari interface, so three of each is the honest answer
# rather than seven Devanagari phrases nobody types. The Devanagari three are the loanword forms that
# are genuinely typed - "टीवी", not "दूरदर्शन".
#
# The seventh slot is left empty on purpose. "टीवी चैनल" was the candidate and belongs almost entirely
# to the IPTV niche; "FM रेडियो" would promise an FM tuner this app is not. Two further candidates were
# invented rather than observed, and six confident terms beat seven with two guesses.
#
# The catalog intent is the weak spot here: "सूची" is the glossary rendering but nobody searches it, so
# the idea is carried by "रेडियो चैनल" instead.
#
# JioTV, Hotstar, SonyLIV, ZEE5, MX Player, Voot, Airtel Xstream, Tata Play, Gaana, Wynk, Hungama,
# Radio Mirchi, Doordarshan and ThopTV were deliberately not used - policy 10.1.3.
